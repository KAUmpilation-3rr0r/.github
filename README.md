## 🚫 What doesn't fly

- 🧊 **No vibe coding.** Looking things up with AI is fine ✅ Letting it write
  your commits is not ❌ And yes, I'm aware of the irony 🙃 I'm allowed. You're
  not
- 🗨 A comment on every single line, checks against things that cannot happen,
  `try/except` wrapped around the universe 🌌 — I can smell it from here
- 🕳 Tokens, keys, IDs, anything private. Not in commits, not in config, not
  "temporarily" 🔒
- 🤐 Code you can't explain. You will be asked. Not as a trap — just because
  someone will have to maintain it 🛠

## 🧾 Touching other people's work

Your own files are yours ✍️ Do what you like, just don't make anyone sad.

Editing something **you didn't write**? 👁 That gets reviewed before it lands.
Normal practice, nothing personal.

And my files 💾 those diffs get read very carefully, line by line. Imagine
someone rewriting your spine 🦴 and pushing it to main without asking. That's
what it feels like.

## 🌱 Adding something new

Own folder, own README, say what it does and how to run it 📝 Two sentences is
enough. Nobody needs your life story — just enough that the next person doesn't
have to guess.

If it's meant for the server, say so in the PR. Then see the DM part above 📬
